The prototypes behind the current design

Every round that was built and reviewed on the way to the current interface, embedded here in full rather than as screenshots — each one is the working specimen, at real size, with real figures. The narrative that connects them is in the design log.

Each study is rendered in its own document. Three of them define the same class names for different things, so merging their stylesheets would break specimens that are currently correct.

02

Four directions

The first comparison pass, and the round where the brand values were finally taken off Kudi's own mark rather than invented. Applied literally, they produced a violet slab. Set in the system stack here — the original specimen shipped custom faces, but this round is about colour and geometry, so they are not worth 250KB.

04

Seven clean treatments

Violet demoted to three appearances per screen. Varies ground, navigation structure, density, and how much colour is permitted at all. Airy and Night turned out to be one design at two luminances.

05

Four island treatments

Panels on a ground instead of sections divided by rules — including the inverse that nearly won, where the shell carries the brand and the content sits inside it.

06

Inset x Deep

Fusing the inset structure with a dark ground. Where the measurement showed two near-blacks never separate by luminance.
